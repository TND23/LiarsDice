Liar's Dice

Play against an AI opponent

Rules:
- The game is played with a five six-sided dice.
- Player's take turns making a bid or calling liar.
- A bid is made by stating how many dice of a certain face value are in the player's hand.
- A player may call liar if they think the other player is lying (the other player must have already made a bid).
- If a player calls liar and is correct, they win the round and the other player loses a die.
- If a player calls liar and is incorrect, they lose a die.
- The game ends when a player loses all their dice.
